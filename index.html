About the practice

Compassionate care, practical strategies

I am Maly Pinhas, a passionate occupational therapist with over 17 years of diverse experience. I hold a master's degree in occupational therapy and have worked extensively across different settings. My experience includes leading a team of therapists at a local institute and running my own clinic where I focused on parent counseling, group sessions, and personalized one-on-one sessions. Currently, my practice primarily supports adults with ADHD through the cognitive-functional (CogFun) approach, which focuses on practical, tailored strategies to improve daily functioning and overall well-being. I am committed to providing compassionate and effective care, helping individuals navigate their unique challenges. If you need support, please don't hesitate to contact me.
